# PRE-REGISTRATION: volatility risk premium, stage 1, measurement (2026-10-06)

Written before any statistic is computed. Frozen at the commit that adds this file together with
`research/vrp.py`.

## Mechanism

Option buyers pay for crash insurance. Across asset classes, implied volatility exceeds the volatility
that is later realised on average. That is the variance risk premium (VRP). A seller of variance
earns it, and pays out in crashes, when realised vol overshoots.

It is documented in equities (Carr & Wu 2009) and, by several studies, in crypto (Deribit BTC/ETH).
It is new data for this repo: no search has touched it.

## Data

- **IV:** Deribit DVOL (30-day implied volatility index, annualised %), BTC and ETH, hourly from
  2021-03-24 (`data/external/deribit_dvol_*_1h.csv`).
- **RV:** realised volatility from Bybit perp hourly closes (`candle_cache`),
  √(Σ r² × 8760 / n) × 100.

## Measurement (fixed now)

- **Sampling:** daily at 00:00 UTC, IV_t = DVOL at that hour; RV_fwd = realised vol over the next 30
  days (720 hourly returns).
- **VRP_t** = IV_t − RV_fwd,t, in vol points. Overlapping daily samples, Newey-West t with 30 lags;
  also non-overlapping 30-day blocks.
- **Short variance swap, vega-notional proxy:** P&L = (IV² − RV²) / (2·IV) vol points per 30-day block.
  Reported per block:
  - mean, Sharpe, skew;
  - the 5 worst blocks with dates;
  - halves.
- **Context, report only:**
  - correlation of block P&L with the BTC block return and with Grid's block P&L (OosCoins trade log);
  - IV − trailing 30d RV as a possible timing variable.

## Cost hurdle (assumption, to be verified with Deribit quote history in stage 2)

**2.5 vol points per 30-day sale held to expiry:**
- about 2 vol points of ATM bid-ask paid once (half on entry; expiry settles free);
- about 0.5 vol points of fees and delta-hedging.

## PASS (stage 1 → stage 2), BOTH BTC and ETH

1. mean VRP > 2.5 vol points with Newey-West t ≥ 2;
2. positive in both halves of the sample;
3. short-variance proxy Sharpe, after subtracting the hurdle per block, > 0.5.

**If PASS:** stage 2 fetches Deribit option quotes and trades a concrete structure (30-day ATM straddle
or strangle, delta-hedged on the perp) under its own pre-registration.

**If FAIL:** the VRP is not worth trading at retail option costs here.

**Trials:** 2 (BTC, ETH).

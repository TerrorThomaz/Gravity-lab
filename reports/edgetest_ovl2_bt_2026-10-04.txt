  WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT SHOW
    · Coins are never-trained, so strategy parameters are out-of-sample on price.
    · The gate is out-of-sample in TIME: fitted only on already-closed trades.
    · The gate is NOT out-of-sample in coin: it is fitted on these same coins'
      realised trades, which is what a live system would also do.
    · Costs are TradeCosts.FeeRoundTripPct + Config.SlippageBps, Bybit-calibrated.
    · No liquidation model. Valid only while gross exposure stays under equity.
    · MarkToMarket accrues an open position's P&L LINEARLY across its hold, so the
      within-hold path is smoothed. That biases Sharpe UP for long-hold strategies
      (the grid family most), and the effect grows with hold length. Compare Sharpe
      across strategies of similar hold length only; CAGR and maxDD are unaffected.
